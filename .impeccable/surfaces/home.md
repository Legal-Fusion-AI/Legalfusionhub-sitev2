# Home surface brief

## Job and audience

- Persuade bankruptcy-firm owners and intake leaders who are losing leads to slow response and inconsistent follow-up.
- Make the managed-service distinction clear: Legal Fusion designs, builds, and runs the acquisition system.

## Outcome and proof

- Primary action: **Book a demo**.
- Prove the mechanism by showing a lead move from first ring through qualification, booking, and signing.
- Use only confirmed evidence: 8,000+ cases filed, 24+ years of legal practice, and practice-born operating experience.

## Selected direction

- **Managed dispatch board:** bright white operational field, deep ink type, electric blue routing paths, coral primary action, and green completed states.
- The hero is split copy plus a large live dispatch surface. A single lead visibly travels through the system; it is not a generic dashboard screenshot.
- Signature interaction: selecting a journey stage reroutes one continuous signal line and updates the active operational panel.
- Direction roll seed: `b21d2ad1`; assigned grounded direction selected under delegated code-led execution.

## Scope and boundaries

- Routes: `/`, `/demo`, `/how-it-works`, `/about`, `/privacy`, `/terms`.
- Light-only and heavily white. No stock photography, generated atmosphere, dark hero, bento grid, pricing, testimonials, or customer logos.
- Legal Fusion Portal remains a small footer product link.

## Interaction and layout

- Desktop hero: message left, dispatch surface right; mobile stacks with the action before the demonstration.
- One orchestrated journey interaction plus restrained control/hover feedback.
- Content stays visible with JavaScript disabled and honors reduced motion.
- HTML/CSS/SVG are the asset medium; no shipping rasters are planned.
