# Legal Fusion Hub

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web marketing site.

## Stack

Delegated implementation: Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, and Vercel.

## Users

Owners, attorneys, intake leaders, and firm administrators at US law firms, especially bankruptcy practices. They arrive while evaluating how to answer every call, turn leads into retained clients, and keep serving those clients inside one company.

## Product Purpose

Legal Fusion Hub is one company for a complete client journey. The public lines are:

- Technology for a stronger practice.
- One company. A complete client journey.
- More than software. A stronger tomorrow for law firms.

The site presents three parts of that journey:

- **Legal Fusion Hub — Engage.** Calls and inquiries get an immediate response 24/7. Hub answers, handles the inquiry, and captures the lead.
- **Legal Fusion Hub — Acquire.** Hub qualifies by the firm’s criteria, follows up, schedules consultations, and helps convert bankruptcy leads into retained clients.
- **Legal Fusion Portal — Serve, Manage, Grow.** After a client is retained, Portal is where the firm and the client continue: questionnaires, documents, case data, and bankruptcy forms. The homepage card links to `/portal`.

The primary website outcome is a qualified firm visitor booking a demo.

## Positioning

Legal Fusion Hub is the company and the system that answers the call and turns the lead into a client. Legal Fusion Portal is the bankruptcy filing application for the work after a client is retained. The company was built from experience inside a bankruptcy practice that has filed more than 8,000 cases across more than 24 years of legal practice. That evidence belongs on About. It is not the homepage promise.

## Product Family

- **Legal Fusion Hub:** the company, this site, and the Engage and Acquire stages.
- **Legal Fusion Portal:** Serve, Manage, and Grow. Introduced at `/portal`. The live app is `https://file.legalfusionhub.com`. The homepage card and navigation link to `/portal`, not to the app.

The former public name “Legal Fusion AI” is retired. Do not publish Client Booker, AI Receptionist, Additional Products, Closebot, or GHL Voice AI. Those names stay internal.

## Core Capabilities

- Answer calls 24/7, handle inquiries, and capture new leads.
- Qualify by the firm’s criteria, follow up, and schedule consultations.
- Help convert bankruptcy leads into retained clients.
- Continue in Portal with questionnaires, documents, case data, and bankruptcy forms.

## Evidence

- Built inside a real bankruptcy practice.
- More than 8,000 cases filed.
- More than 24 years of legal practice.
- Prior internal system evidence includes 500 automated actions in one week.

Do not invent customer logos, testimonials, pricing, certifications, conversion rates, or performance benchmarks.

## Primary Action

Use the exact label **Book a demo** throughout the site. It leads to `/demo`.

The demo request captures name, work email, firm, role, and optional notes. It sends to a configured GoHighLevel endpoint when available and otherwise opens a pre-addressed email.

## Brand Commitments

- Light, heavily white SaaS presentation.
- White or near-white owns most of every page.
- No dark-first theme, full-bleed dark hero, or warm cream-paper visual world.
- Ink or navy may carry typography and chrome, but not dominate the page ground.
- The experience should feel specific to one company with a complete client journey, not legal-editorial, generic AI, or consumer bankruptcy education.
- Avoid purple/indigo AI meshes and image-heavy generated atmospheres.
- Prefer interactive HTML, CSS, and SVG product demonstrations over stock or generated imagery.

## Voice

Direct, specific, calm, and commercially clear. Use the owner’s product language: calls, leads, consultations, retained clients, and the stages Engage, Acquire, and Serve, Manage, Grow. The closer is “more than software.” Avoid startup hype, vague AI claims, and claims that automation replaces the firm’s human judgment.

## Constraints

- Public copy is for law firms, not debtors seeking legal advice.
- The website does not provide legal advice.
- Portal is a named product on the homepage and at `/portal`. Do not describe it as billing, communication, or post-bankruptcy credit.
- No public pricing in the initial release.
- Accessibility, responsive behavior, reduced-motion support, and production-ready form states are required.
- Do not publish vendor or retired product names. Public names are Legal Fusion Hub and Legal Fusion Portal.
