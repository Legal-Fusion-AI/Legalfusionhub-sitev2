# Legal Fusion Hub

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web marketing site.

## Stack

Delegated implementation: Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, and Vercel.

## Users

Owners, attorneys, intake leaders, and firm administrators at US bankruptcy law firms. They arrive while evaluating how to answer every lead, follow up consistently, and turn more inquiries into booked consultations without adding fragmented software or manual work.

## Product Purpose

Legal Fusion Hub is a done-for-you client acquisition system for bankruptcy law firms. It answers and qualifies leads, books consultations, nurtures prospects, coordinates intake and signing, and supports review generation. Legal Fusion designs, implements, manages, and improves the system for each firm.

The primary website outcome is a qualified firm visitor booking a demo.

## Positioning

Legal Fusion Hub is not another self-serve intake tool or generic marketing agency. It combines managed implementation with bankruptcy-practice operating knowledge. The system was built from experience inside a bankruptcy practice that has filed more than 8,000 cases across more than 24 years of legal practice.

## Product Family

- **Legal Fusion Hub:** done-for-you client acquisition system and the subject of this site.
- **Legal Fusion Portal:** bankruptcy filing application at `https://file.legalfusionhub.com`. It appears as a secondary product link, not as the homepage offer.

The former public name “Legal Fusion AI” is retired as the primary product name for this experience.

## Core Capabilities

- Answer inbound leads around the clock.
- Qualify prospects according to the firm’s criteria.
- Book consultations and send confirmations.
- Collect intake information and coordinate next steps.
- Nurture leads who are not ready immediately.
- Support digital signing and review generation.
- Give firms visibility into the acquisition journey.

## Evidence

- Built inside a real bankruptcy practice.
- More than 8,000 cases filed.
- More than 24 years of legal practice.
- Prior internal system evidence includes 500 automated actions in one week.

Do not invent customer logos, testimonials, pricing, certifications, conversion rates, or performance benchmarks.

## Primary Action

Use the exact label **Book a demo** throughout the site. It leads to `/demo`.

The demo request captures name, work email, firm, role, and optional notes. It sends to a configured GoHighLevel endpoint when available and otherwise opens a pre-addressed email.

## Brand Commitments

- Light, heavily white SaaS presentation.
- White or near-white owns most of every page.
- No dark-first theme, full-bleed dark hero, or warm cream-paper visual world.
- Ink or navy may carry typography and chrome, but not dominate the page ground.
- The experience should feel specific to a modern managed acquisition system, not legal-editorial, generic AI, or consumer bankruptcy education.
- Avoid purple/indigo AI meshes and image-heavy generated atmospheres.
- Prefer interactive HTML, CSS, and SVG product demonstrations over stock or generated imagery.

## Voice

Direct, specific, calm, and commercially clear. Use the vocabulary of firm operations: leads, calls, qualification, consultations, intake, signing, and cases. Avoid startup hype, vague AI claims, and claims that automation replaces the firm’s human judgment.

## Constraints

- Public copy is for law firms, not debtors seeking legal advice.
- The website does not provide legal advice.
- Keep Portal positioning secondary in this phase.
- No public pricing in the initial release.
- Accessibility, responsive behavior, reduced-motion support, and production-ready form states are required.
